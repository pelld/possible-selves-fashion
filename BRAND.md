# Current Torunkëshk direction

Torunkëshk is a provisional name for a premium clothing and footwear brand built around the idea of possible selves. The customer is not shown a warehouse of products. The shop pays attention, offers two considered directions, and gradually behaves like a stylist.

The visual world begins almost colourless. Human figures are faint, neutral outlines without faces or judgements about beauty or body shape. The clothes supply the colour, texture and character. Deep petrol and black-cherry plum are the principal colours, with muted violet behind them, warm white trim, and quiet grey stitching.

The material should feel soft and substantial rather than glossy or technical: sueded heavyweight cotton jersey, peached cotton, or a similarly brushed fabric that holds its shape. The defining garment detail is a double grey stitch line at the lower hem. The diaeresis from the `ë` becomes a small two-dot signature positioned above the stitching near the bottom-left of the garment.

The wordmark uses a double-line geometric structure. It remains pale and architectural, while small sections between the lines fill with colour in a slow, irregular pattern. The movement should never look like an advert, a loading animation or a glitch. It should feel as though the brand is quietly becoming present.
