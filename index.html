A first brand direction

Clothes for possible selves.

Soft in the hand. Certain in shape. The stylist begins with you, then takes a position.

Take your time.

The material language

Plush, not precious.

Sueded heavyweight cotton jersey gives the softness of brushed fabric without losing its structure. White trim makes the dark colours feel clean rather than heavy.

Shape Relaxed, but held.
Touch Brushed cotton.
Signature Two stitches. Two dots.

The quiet mark

There when you look for it.

The diaeresis from the name becomes the smallest possible logo: two dots near the lower-left hem, held above two grey stitch lines. It repeats across the clothes and the website without shouting.

double grey stitching / white trim / brushed surface

The palette

Colour with restraint.

Petrol and black cherry do the heavy lifting. Violet sits behind them as a quieter bridge. White trim and stitch grey keep the whole system clear.

Working proposition

Made for now. Built for next.

Torunkëshk is provisional. The design language is the real work: possible selves, attentive service, plush structure, restrained colour, and the mark hidden in plain sight.
